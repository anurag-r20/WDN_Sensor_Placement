EPANET file: NJ1.inp
The NJ1_WDN_Nodes, _Edges and _Water_Consumption TXT files are converted from the EPANET file. Every junction, reservoir and tank is a node and every pipe, pump and valve is an edge. Bare-number IDs get a J- (junction) or T- (reservoir or tank) prefix. Consumption is the junction's base demand, or the sum of its [DEMANDS] entries when it has any.
